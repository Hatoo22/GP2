How Dir'a works

From thousands of player comments to one clear score, in five steps.

  1. 1

    We gather what players say

    Player reviews from Steam, comments from YouTube, and what players share and report here on Dir'a.

  2. 2

    We keep only what matters

    Links, repeats and one-word comments are filtered out, so the AI only reads real opinions.

  3. 3

    AI reads every comment

    Our fine-tuned ModernBERT model decides if a comment is toxic and which behaviors it shows. One comment can show more than one.

    BullyingHate speechSexual harassmentThreatsOther toxicity
  4. 4

    Serious behaviors count more

    We find the share of comments showing each behavior, give each one a weight, and add them up into one risk percentage.

    • Sexual harassment26.6%
    • Hate speech20.1%
    • Bullying19.5%
    • Threats18.2%
    • Other toxicity15.6%
  5. 5

    You get one clear score

    Every game gets a risk percentage and a level. When players share their experience on Dir'a, the score updates right away.

    Low · up to 30% Medium · 31–69% High · 70%+
