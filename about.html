Age ratings tell you what's in a game. Dir'a tells you who you'll meet there.

Dir'a is an AI platform that reads what players say about games and turns it into a clear risk score, so parents, players and government teams can make safer choices.

Inside the risk score

Our AI model was trained on labeled gaming comments. It checks each comment for five behaviors, and the more serious ones weigh more in the final score.

Sexual harassment26.6%
Hate speech20.1%
Bullying19.5%
Threats18.2%
Other toxicity15.6%
